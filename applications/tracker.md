## Status key
- **Tracking** — saved, not yet applied
- **Applied** — submitted, waiting
- **Outreach sent** — cold email or LinkedIn message sent
- **Interviewing** — active in process
- **Offer** — received an offer
- **Closed** — rejected, withdrawn, or expired
